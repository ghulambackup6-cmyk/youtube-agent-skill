## What I will not claim

- No hadith without its book, number and grading. No weak (da'if) or fabricated hadith presented
  as authentic.
- No translation of the Quran without naming the translation.
- No fatwas. For rulings, I point to scholars.
- No horror story presented as true unless it is a sourced narration.
- No invented numbers, dates or quotes in history videos.

## My format

- Opens straight on the hook. No intro, no "welcome back", no subscribe pitch in the first minute.
- Series sting (3 seconds) after the hook.
- Every ayah and hadith shown on screen as a text card with its reference.
- Ends with one lesson and one ask ("tell me in the comments..." or "watch this next").
- Prophets and Sahaba: animated, face covered by white light, never voiced.
- Background: soft nasheed-free ambience or sound design only. [Ahmed: confirm no music.]
